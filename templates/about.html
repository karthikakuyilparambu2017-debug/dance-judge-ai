About Dance Judge AI

Dance Judge AI is an innovative artificial intelligence system designed to analyze Bharatanatyam dance performances. Using computer vision and AI models, it evaluates dancers based on:

Key Features

  • 🎭 Synchronization Analysis: Evaluates how well two or more dancers move together.
  • 💄 Makeup Assessment: Checks for traditional Bharatanatyam makeup elements like eye and lip highlights.
  • 👗 Costume Evaluation: Identifies whether the attire follows Bharatanatyam's traditional dress code.
  • 📹 Real-Time Video Processing: Works with uploaded or live-streamed performances.
  • 📊 Score-Based Feedback: Provides numerical scores and insights for improvement.

How It Works

Dance Judge AI uses **computer vision and AI-powered algorithms** to analyze dance videos. Here's the process:

  1. Step 1: Upload a Bharatanatyam dance video.
  2. Step 2: The AI extracts skeleton-based motion data using **MediaPipe**.
  3. Step 3: It compares dancers’ movements to measure synchronization accuracy.
  4. Step 4: Facial recognition detects Bharatanatyam makeup features.
  5. Step 5: Costume detection ensures traditional Bharatanatyam attire is worn.
  6. Step 6: The system generates a **final report with scores**.
